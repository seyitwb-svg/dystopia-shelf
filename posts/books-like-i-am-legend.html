7 books like I Am Legend: last men and what comes at night

Reading list · By Max, the reader behind Dystopia Shelf · Last updated: October 8, 2026

← Dystopia Shelf

Matheson's novel works because Neville isn't a hero — he's a man keeping a schedule in an emptied city, checking the doors before dark. These six inherit that loneliness at different scales: one survivor, one species, one continent at a time. The seventh charges the plague rent.

1. Earth Abides · George R. Stewart (1949)

A geologist comes down from the mountains to find a pandemic has emptied America, then spends decades watching roads, water systems, and eventually words themselves wash out. No monsters at all — just entropy. Published 1949.

2. Oryx and Crake · Margaret Atwood (2003)

Snowman lives in a tree after a bioengineered plague, keeping watch over the placid children his friend Crake designed to replace humanity. The survivor story as autopsy — Atwood opens the whole catastrophe piece by piece. Published 2003.

3. World War Z · Max Brooks (2006)

The zombie war told entirely as interviews — a UN researcher collecting testimonies from survivors on every continent. Matheson's isolation scaled to a species that had to lose before it adapted. Published 2006.

4. The Girl with All the Gifts · M.R. Carey (2014)

A fungal infection turns its hosts into 'hungries' — except the children, who attend lessons on a military base between restraints. The plague's point of view, literally: Melanie is both specimen and the kindest character in the book. Published 2014.

5. The Day of the Triffids · John Wyndham (1951)

A meteor shower blinds nearly everyone on Earth in one night — and the walking carnivorous plants humanity cultivated for oil start feeding instead. Wyndham's survivors rebuild in committees and communes while something very patient waits outside. Published 1951.

6. The Passage · Justin Cronin (2010)

A military experiment meant to extend life releases twelve test subjects who carry a vampire plague across the continent; the last hundred pages jump a century ahead to the walled colony their descendants built. Published 2010.

7. My Brother on the Moon · Maximilian Coolheit Vorfahrenheit (2025)

Disclosure: I wrote My Brother on the Moon. About 68 pages of dystopia where the plague still harvests one hour a day of every citizen. A gravedigger who just felt the sickle; a stranger still on the vine. $2.99, KU: amazon.com/dp/B0G1KNPF4X.

FAQ

What book is most similar to I Am Legend?

Oryx and Crake — the same premise of one survivor cataloguing a plague that rewrote humanity, told with Atwood's colder eye. For the siege rhythm specifically, The Passage.

What should I read if I liked I Am Legend?

Earth Abides for the quiet version, World War Z for the documentary scale, The Girl with All the Gifts for the plague seen from inside its carriers.

Is there a short book like I Am Legend?

My Brother on the Moon runs about 68 pages — a town where the plague still harvests one hour a day of every citizen.
