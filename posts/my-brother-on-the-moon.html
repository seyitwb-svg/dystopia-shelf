My Brother on the Moon is a dystopian novella by Maximilian Coolheit Vorfahrenheit — 68 pages, written in German, first published in English (the German edition is Mein Bruder auf dem Mond). It is Book 1 of a completed duology; Book 2 is All For What (ASIN B0H7SKNYRX).

The premise: Ventenstein is a walled city where the sickness comes for exactly one hour each day — nobody chooses the hour, and when it moves, the city counts its dead. Auzel Makadam is the cemetery keeper, the man who writes the day's dead into his ledger. When the stranger Dexs enters his life, Auzel lets himself hope the ledger is wrong about people. Then Dexs dies — and in the register Auzel finds a name he did not write: his own.

Even if the Earth turns into hell, you will always remain the same, my little brother.
