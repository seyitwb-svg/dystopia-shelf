7 books like Children of Men, where the future got cancelled

Reading list · By Max, the reader behind Dystopia Shelf · Last updated: October 7, 2026

← Dystopia Shelf

P.D. James's Omega generation never got younger — no births, no bargaining, just a species aging out while the state keeps the paperwork tidy. These six inherit the same premise from different angles: reproduction as policy, bodies as resource, and futures the system reposeses. Plus one indie where the cancelled future isn't a generation but a daily hour.

1. The Testament of Jessie Lamb · Jane Rogers (2011)

A virus sterilizes humanity; the experimental fix is a pregnancy that kills the mother. Jessie, sixteen, volunteers anyway — and the novel becomes a locked-room argument between a girl and the parents trying to save her from her own hope. Won the Arthur C. Clarke Award. Published 2011.

2. The Book of the Unnamed Midwife · Meg Elison (2014)

A fever wipes out nearly every woman on earth; the unnamed midwife survives by dressing as a man and hoarding contraceptives for the women left. Elison's plague world is the rawest entry here — the future didn't just get cancelled, it got hunted. Philip K. Dick Award winner. Published 2014.

3. The Farm · Joanne Ramos (2019)

Golden Oaks looks like a spa: surrogates — mostly immigrant women — carry rich clients' babies under perfect nutrition and total surveillance. The future cancelled here isn't children but ownership: luxury captivity priced by the month. Published 2019.

4. When She Woke · Hillary Jordan (2011)

In a puritan near-future, criminals aren't jailed — they're chromed: skin dyed the color of their crime and released back into the public eye. Hannah's abortion turns her red, and her sentence is her skin. The Scarlet Letter, weaponized. Published 2011.

5. The Salt Line · Holly Goddard Jones (2017)

America lives behind a quarantine border that keeps disease-carrying ticks out; rich tourists pay to zip-line into the abandoned wild beyond it. The cancelled future is out there — the tour just takes you into it. Published 2017.

6. The School for Good Mothers · Jessamine Chan (2022)

After one bad afternoon, Frida's daughter is removed by the state and she's remanded to a re-education facility where parenting is scored against robot dolls. The future doesn't end in a bang — it's measured out of you, one metric at a time. Published 2022.

7. My Brother on the Moon · Maximilian Coolheit Vorfahrenheit (2025)

Disclosure: I wrote My Brother on the Moon. About 68 pages of cancelled futures on the installment plan: in Ventenstein every citizen's future is rented out by the hour — the sickness forecloses on an hour a day. A gravedigger just got his eviction notice; a stranger pays no rent. $2.99, KU: amazon.com/dp/B0G1KNPF4X.

FAQ

What should I read after Children of Men?

The Testament of Jessie Lamb for the same 'no more children' premise turned inward; The Book of the Unnamed Midwife for a rawer survival take; The Farm for the corporate version of reproductive control.

What is the closest book to Children of Men?

The Testament of Jessie Lamb — mass sterility again, but filtered through a teenage narrator who volunteers for the dangerous fix. James's geopolitics become one family's kitchen table.

Is there a short book like Children of Men?

My Brother on the Moon runs about 68 pages — no children aren't the issue; time itself is. A plague steals one violent hour a day from every citizen, and the gravedigger keeping count finds his own name in the ledger.
